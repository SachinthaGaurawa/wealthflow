Your money, in one calm, private place.

WealthFlow is a personal-finance app built for Sri Lankan households. It reads the bank statements that land in your own Gmail, files every transaction with the help of several independent AI models, and keeps your loans, credit cards, cheques and bills in one dashboard, with your debt-service coverage (DSCR) always up to date.

What WealthFlow does

Built around the way Sri Lankan banking really works

Statements arrive by email, cards and cheques are everywhere, and loans have instalments. WealthFlow brings them together and does the filing for you.

Autonomous email sync

Connect your Gmail once, read-only, and approve the bank senders you trust. New statements are found and filed automatically, with a scheduled check as a safety net. Only mail from the senders you approve is looked at.

Cross-checked by several AIs

Instead of trusting one model, a transaction that the rules cannot place is put to several independent AI providers at the same time. A row is filed only when they agree; anything disputed is left for you to review.

DSCR calculator

See your debt-service coverage ratio, income against loan and card obligations, as a clear gauge with plain-language advice, so you know how much room you really have.

Statement scanning

Upload a bank or card statement PDF and WealthFlow reads the rows, recognises the bank, and refuses duplicate uploads so nothing is counted twice.

Loans, cards, cheques & bills

Track loan instalments, credit-card limits and due dates, cheques from issue to clearing, and recurring subscriptions, with reminders before things fall due.

AI financial advisor

Ask questions about your own finances. The advisor is given the same figures your dashboard shows, so its answers match what you see on screen.

How it works

From inbox to insight in four steps

  1. Sign in with GoogleUse your Google account to sign in. The first time, WealthFlow creates your private workspace from your name and email.
  2. Connect what you chooseOptionally link Gmail (read-only) for statements and Google Drive for backups. Both can be disconnected at any time.
  3. WealthFlow files itRules and several AI models sort income, expenses, cards and loans. You review only what is genuinely unclear.
  4. You stay in controlDashboards, DSCR, reminders and an advisor that works from your real numbers, behind your own PIN or biometric lock.
Your data

Your financial data is yours

A short version of what the Privacy Policy explains in full.

What we access

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, to create and open your account.
  • Gmail (optional, read-only): statement emails from the bank senders you approve, to file them for you. WealthFlow never sends, deletes or changes mail.
  • Google Drive (optional): only files WealthFlow itself creates, for your backups.
  • What you add: incomes, expenses, loans, cards, cheques and statements you upload.

How it is protected

  • Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS; data is stored in Google Firebase with per-user access rules, so one account cannot read another's.
  • Gmail access tokens live only on the server, in a store that browsers cannot read.
  • Some on-device data and the bank-password vault are encrypted on your device before they are stored.
  • No advertising, no data sales, no tracking pixels.
Google API Services User Data Policy. WealthFlow's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Gmail is used only to provide the statement-filing feature you can see in the app.
