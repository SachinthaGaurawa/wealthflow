WealthFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to www.wealthflow.lk, the WealthFlow web app and its installed (PWA) versions

In short. WealthFlow is a personal-finance app. To work, it stores the financial records you enter or import, and, only if you choose to connect them, reads bank-statement emails from your Gmail and keeps backup files in your Google Drive. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features you can see in the app (see section 3). You can disconnect Google, export or delete your data, and ask us to erase your account at any time (sections 10 to 12).

1. Who we are

WealthFlow is a personal-finance application for households in Sri Lanka, published at https://www.wealthflow.lk (the “Service”). It is operated by Sachintha Gaurawa (“WealthFlow”, “we”, “us”), who is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it goes and what control you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

WealthFlow is a record-keeping and information tool. It is not a bank, lender or licensed financial adviser.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account details. The name, email address and profile picture supplied by your Google account when you sign in, or the email address you register with. If you set a PIN or biometric unlock, it is used to lock the app on your device and to derive encryption keys there.
  • Financial records you enter or import. Income, expenses, balances, loans and instalments, credit cards, cheques, bills and subscriptions, pawn and debtor records, savings targets, and the people and payment details you save (for example a payee’s name and account number).
  • Documents you upload. Bank or card statements (PDF, image or text), photographs of receipts and the transactions read from them.
  • Settings and preferences, including notification choices, categories and rules you teach the app.
  • Feedback you send through the in-app feedback form, including the email address you give.

2.2 Information from Google, only if you allow it

  • Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication): your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account identifier.
  • Gmail (optional). If you connect Gmail, WealthFlow requests read-only access. It looks only at messages from the bank and card senders you have approved in the app, to find statement and transaction-alert emails and file them for you. WealthFlow does not send, delete, label or change any mail, and does not read mail from senders you have not approved.
  • Google Drive (optional). If you connect Drive for backups, WealthFlow uses the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files that WealthFlow itself creates. It cannot browse the rest of your Drive. A separate, off-by-default setting (“Clean Drive file browser”) lets you browse your own Drive folders to pick a statement file to import. Only if you switch it on, WealthFlow asks Google for the read-only drive.readonly permission, in its own consent step. It is used only to list the file and folder names you navigate and to download the one file you choose; WealthFlow does not change, delete or upload anything with it, does not scan the rest of your Drive, and does not keep copies of files you did not pick.

2.3 Information collected through use

  • Technical data that any web service receives: IP address, browser and device type, and request time. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard request and error logs. We use these logs to keep the Service running and to find faults, not to profile you.
  • Operational records, such as when a Gmail sync last ran, whether an import succeeded, which statements have already been filed (so duplicates are refused), and error diagnostics.

2.4 SMS (optional)

If the owner of an account switches on SMS notifications, WealthFlow can send text messages (for example payment reminders or alerts) through the Text.lk SMS gateway. This uses the mobile numbers that the account owner has entered, and the message content, delivery status and credit balance are recorded so the feature can be audited and kept within its limits. WealthFlow does not read your SMS inbox.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, tracking pixels or third-party advertising cookies. We do not collect your precise location, contacts or photo library; you only share a file or photo when you choose it. Passwords of your bank accounts are never required to use WealthFlow; if you choose to store a bank password in the optional vault, it is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your PIN before it is saved (see section 7).

3. Google user data and Limited Use

WealthFlow’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, for the data we receive from Google APIs (including Gmail message content and Google Drive files):

  • Use is limited to the visible features. Gmail data is used only to identify statement and transaction emails from senders you approved and turn them into entries in your own WealthFlow account. Drive access is used only to store and restore your WealthFlow backup. Sign-in data is used only to create and open your account.
  • No transfer except as needed. We do not transfer Google user data to others except as necessary to provide or improve these user-facing features (for example, processing by our hosting and AI providers described in section 5 and section 6), to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or sale of the Service with notice to you.
  • No advertising. We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • No human reading. Our people do not read your Gmail content. An exception exists only when you explicitly send it to us for support, when it is necessary for security or abuse investigations, or when the law requires it.
  • No use to train general AI models. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models. Where parts of an email are sent to an AI provider to read a transaction (section 5), it is only to produce the result shown to you, under that provider’s API terms.
  • Server-side token storage. When you connect Gmail, the long-lived access (refresh) token is kept on our server in a store that browsers and other users cannot read. You can disconnect at any time (section 11).

4. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: show your dashboards, reminders and DSCR figures, and keep your data in sync between your devices.
  • To file transactions automatically: read statements you upload or that arrive from approved senders, recognise the bank, classify each row, and refuse duplicate uploads.
  • To answer your questions in the AI financial advisor, using the figures from your own account.
  • To send messages you asked for: reminders, alerts, one-time sign-in codes and replies to your feedback.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not make decisions about you that have legal effects. Classifications and advice are suggestions that you can review and change.

5. AI processing

WealthFlow uses artificial-intelligence models for tasks such as reading a statement or receipt, classifying a transaction, spotting recurring bills and answering advisor questions. For transaction classification the service can put a question to several independent AI providers and accept an answer only where they agree.

  • What is sent. Only what a task needs: for classification, the transaction description, merchant text and amount; for statement or receipt reading, the text or image of that document; for the advisor, your question and a summary of the figures from your own account. We do not send your sign-in details, PIN or stored bank passwords.
  • Who receives it. AI providers we configure from time to time, currently including Google (Gemini), Mistral, Cohere, DeepSeek, Together AI, Fireworks, NVIDIA, Groq, OpenRouter, Cloudflare Workers AI, GitHub Models and Ollama-hosted models. The set changes as providers are added or retired; ask us for the current list at any time.
  • Terms. We use these providers through their API services. We do not authorise them to use content we send for advertising. Each provider’s own terms govern its handling of API requests.
  • Merchant lookup. To name an unknown merchant we may run a web search that contains only the merchant name and a country hint, never your account data.

7. How we protect your data

We describe our safeguards as they actually work, so that you can judge them accurately.

  • Encryption in transit. The Service is served over HTTPS with HTTP Strict Transport Security.
  • Access control. Your records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under security rules that let a signed-in user read and write only their own documents. Records used by our server-side features (for example the Gmail connection, the inbox of parsed emails and SMS logs) sit in collections that browsers cannot read or write at all; only our server code can.
  • Encryption at rest. Data stored with Google Firebase and Vercel is encrypted at rest by those providers.
  • Additional on-device encryption. Selected sensitive items are encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before they are stored, using keys derived with PBKDF2 (for example the optional bank-password vault, which is protected by a key derived from your PIN). Your PIN and biometric unlock protect the app on your device.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted. WealthFlow is not a zero-knowledge service. To provide features such as automatic statement filing, AI classification and reminders, our servers and the providers in section 6 process your data in readable form. We therefore make no end-to-end encryption claim for the Service as a whole.
  • Least privilege for Google. Gmail access is read-only and limited to approved senders; Drive access is limited to files WealthFlow creates.
  • Operational care. Secrets are kept in server environment variables, not in the app; shared statement links expire after 30 days; and automated checks run on every change to our code.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal data we will notify affected users and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Cookies, local storage and the service worker

WealthFlow does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores data in your browser using local storage, IndexedDB and a service worker so that the app works quickly and partly offline, remembers your settings and PIN-lock state, and keeps you signed in. Firebase Authentication also keeps sign-in state in your browser. These items are necessary for the Service to function. You can clear them through your browser settings, which signs you out of that device.

9. Retention

  • Your financial records are kept for as long as your account exists, so that your history and DSCR figures stay available.
  • Gmail connection details are kept until you disconnect Gmail or ask us to erase your account.
  • Shared statement links expire automatically after 30 days.
  • Restore points (backups kept in your account). If you switch the Auto-Backup Schedule on, WealthFlow saves a full copy of your records in your own account at 23:55 (or when you press “Backup Now”), and a scheduled server job saves the same copy overnight for accounts whose schedule is on, even when the app is closed. We keep the newest five, the newest of each of the last 14 days, the fullest copy of the last 60 days, and the safety copies taken just before a restore or a reset for 30 days; older ones are deleted automatically. You can delete all of them yourself in Settings (Restore Vault, “Delete all restore points”), and they are removed when your account is erased.
  • Backups in your Google Drive. If you connect Drive, a backup file is saved in a “WealthFlow Backups” folder in your own Drive, and the newest six are kept; older ones are moved to your Drive trash. They belong to your Google account.
  • Server logs kept by our hosting provider are retained for the periods that provider sets.
  • After a deletion request we remove or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep specific records by law, or in backups that are overwritten on their normal cycle.

10. Your choices and rights

You can at any time view and edit your records in the app, export or back them up, turn optional features (Gmail, Drive, SMS, notifications) on or off, and delete entries.

Under Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, and, where it applies to you, the laws of the place where you live, you may have the right to be informed about how your data is processed, to access it, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have it erased, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to the supervisory authority. To use any of these rights, write to us using the details in section 17. We will answer within 30 days and may need to confirm that the request comes from the account owner.

11. Disconnecting Google

  • In WealthFlow. Use the disconnect option for Gmail or Google Drive in the app’s settings. Gmail disconnection removes the stored access token from our server.
  • In your Google account. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose WealthFlow and select “Delete all connections” (or “Remove access”). Access ends at once and does not depend on us.

Disconnecting stops new reading of your mail or Drive. Entries already filed in WealthFlow stay in your account until you delete them.

12. Deleting your data

  • In the app. You can delete individual records, and the “Factory reset” option in settings erases your financial records from the device and from your live cloud copy. It keeps your sign-in so you can start again. It first saves a safety copy in your account, kept for 30 days so a mistake can be undone; restore points you already have also stay until they rotate out (see section 9) unless you delete them in Restore Vault.
  • Full account erasure. To have your account and the data we hold about you on our servers (including the Gmail connection, parsed-email inbox and SMS logs) deleted, email us from the address you signed up with. We will confirm and complete it within 30 days.
  • Backups in your own Drive. Backup files WealthFlow created in your Google Drive belong to your Google account; delete them from Drive when you no longer want them.

13. Information about other people

You may record details about other people, such as a tenant, a debtor or a payee (name, mobile number, amounts owed). You are responsible for having a proper reason to hold that information and for using it only for your own record-keeping. If you use the optional tenant statement portal, WealthFlow creates a private link that shows that person a statement of what they owe, protected by a long unguessable token and not indexed by search engines. If you appear in someone’s records and want it corrected or removed, contact that account holder or write to us.

14. International processing

Our providers operate data centres in several countries, so your information may be processed outside Sri Lanka, including in the United States and the European Union. Where we transfer personal data we rely on the safeguards available under the applicable law, such as contractual protections with the provider.

15. Children

WealthFlow is intended for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes or the law requires. The “Last updated” date above shows the current version. If a change is material, for example if we begin to use Google data in a new way, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and where Google or the law requires it we will ask for your consent again.
